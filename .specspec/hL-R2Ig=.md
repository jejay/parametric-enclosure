# The red half is a lid thrown open by a torsion spring

The red half is a 2 mm dome hinged at the back. One torsion spring on the hinge axis (Gutekunst T-16324, 33 N mm at 115° of wind-up, 80 % of its rated torque) acts over the whole travel. It throws the lid open against 15 N mm of gravity and still holds it against the end stop at 95° with 6 N mm. With a spring at the low end of its tolerance, 12 N mm remain to open.

Why a torsion spring on the hinge axis: it also holds the lid open in any orientation, which a plunger near the hinge does not. Why 95°: beyond 63° gravity helps, and the ring is fully visible from the front.

Origin: owner-suggested spring mechanism; agent conclusion for type and sizing (research/06_mechanics_printing_ring.md, section 2.1). In revision three the owner weighed a redesign round an assortment of torsion springs sold on Amazon (Swpeet, wire of 1 to 2.5 mm) and kept this spring.
