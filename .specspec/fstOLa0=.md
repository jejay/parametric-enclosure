# Recorded Tonie messages play forever without the app

The message recorded for each Tonie is stored persistently in the internal memory of the device. Presenting the matching Tonie to the device identifies it by its RFID tag, just like a real Toniebox does, and plays its message from internal memory. No smartphone and no accompanying app are involved in this.

Where the RFID reader sits and how a Tonie is presented is settled in the requirements below this one.

Origin: owner intention.
