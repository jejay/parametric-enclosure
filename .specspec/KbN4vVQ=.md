# The hole of the sleeve is found by trying; every sleeve file is named after its sizes

Which blind hole holds a pin of 2 mm depends on printer and filament, and the owner does not report back which one fitted. The files and the manual therefore have to carry the answer for whoever prints: the sleeve is exported once for each of the holes 1.85, 1.90, 1.95, 2.00 and 2.05 mm, every sleeve file carries the outside diameter and the hole of its sleeve in its name (sleeve_4_0_hole_1_95 is 4.0 mm outside with holes of 1.95 mm), and the building manual says that the size has to be figured out, and how.

How, in the manual: print all sizes in one job, two of each, and keep them apart by the order of the job, because the sizes cannot be told apart by eye; the right size is the largest hole into which a pin goes only under firm pressure from pliers, down to its bottom, and from which it cannot be pulled with the fingers; the sleeve that goes into the hinge is one in which no pin has been, because a mounted sleeve can only be cut out.

Why sleeves and not the test piece: a post of the test piece is as large a print as a sleeve, so the owner tries the sleeves themselves. The test piece stays for the sliding bores of the towers, which are part of the stage.

What is known so far: the sleeve of revision six, a pipe with a through hole of 2.30 mm, is too loose for the pins, and the sleeves with holes of 1.90 and 1.85 mm took no pin on the owner's printer. Whether the sleeve of 1.95 mm was among those printed, and which of 2.00 and 2.05 mm holds, is not recorded. The 1.95 mm of HINGE_PRESS are the value the model is drawn and checked with: a starting value, not a finding.

Limits: the thicker sleeve for springs of 5.3 to 5.6 mm inside is exported in the one hole size of the model. A sleeve printed alone stays too hot and comes out thin with closed holes, as the owner's first one did; the manual says to print several at once.

Origin: owner decision of 2026-11-25: names that contain the size, and a manual that says the size has to be figured out. The naming scheme, the five sizes as a set and the way of trying them are the agent's.
