# A pawl holds the lid; the servo only pushes it aside

A hook under the front of the lid reaches through the stage. A pawl turning on a vertical pivot under the ring deck lies over the hook's tooth. The lid's force on the pawl is parallel to the pivot and produces no torque, so the latch holds without the servo and the servo never carries load.

To open, the trimmed arm of a 9 g servo pushes a post on the pawl's tail and turns it by 24°; a small spring returns it. To close, the lid is pressed down: the sloped tooth pushes the pawl aside and it snaps back.

The force on the pawl is 0.3 N. The servo delivers more than 10 N at the post.
