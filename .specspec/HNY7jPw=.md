# The front button is a light, not a button

The "button" does not need to be a functioning push button, because the ball only opens on the trigger from the smartphone. Instead the button, and possibly the white flat rim it sits on inside the black rim, is transparent and backed by an RGB LED.

The light signals GPS closeness to the next Tonie location by blinking a certain number of times or at a certain frequency.

Origin: owner intention; blinking by count or frequency is the owner's suggested signal and stays open to the later app and firmware work.
