# The Tonie stands free on a plain flat spot

Some actual grass may surround the place where the Tonie stands, but the surface it stands on is just flat: no locking mechanism and no inset. Whoever places a Tonie there must not get the feeling that everything holds together in the hand without holding the Tonie.

One day, when everything stands on a shelf, the Tonie can stand on the flat spot of the grass on its own.

The "error-zone" may be enlarged a bit where that is needed, for example by shrinking the grass. The agent reads this as the bare ground round the spot, within which a figure may stand off centre.

Origin: owner intention, revision two; the allowance for the error-zone is the owner's, revision three.
