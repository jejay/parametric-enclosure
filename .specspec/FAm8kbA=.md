# The hinge is inside; a shallow pocket in the band is its only trace

The hinge axis lies inside the lid, 7 mm above the seam and 5 mm inside the surface; its pins are carried by two towers of the inner stage. Nothing of it shows on the closed ball.

An axis inside the shell makes the lid's rear rim swing down and inwards before it rises. The top edge of the black band is therefore lowered by 2.75 mm over the rear 48 mm, following the exact surface the rim sweeps. That pocket is black, lies under the overhang of the lid and can be closed from inside with a strip of felt that the rim pushes aside. The stage is cut back around the hinge for the same sweep, and beside the hinge for the lip of the lid, which swings inwards before it rises.

Alternatives examined: a hinge barrel outside the shell needs no pocket but announces that the ball opens. Guiding the lid in arc slots about a virtual pivot on the rim needs no pocket either, but the pins would react the spring force at a large radius and the mechanism would bind by friction.

Origin: agent conclusion.
