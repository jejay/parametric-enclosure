# Emergency opening with a paper clip through a sound slit

If the electronics fail, a straightened paper clip pushed 21 mm into the upper left sound slit, rising at 22°, leaves the bezel through a slot in its wall and pushes a lug on the pawl: the lid opens. No hole, cover or screw exists for this purpose; the entry is one of the four slits the speaker needs anyway.

A magnetic release was rejected because every Tonie carries a magnet.

Origin: agent conclusion.
