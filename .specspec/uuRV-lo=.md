# Inner stage: the ring deck 28 mm above the centre

Under the lid lies a second, black dome 2 mm inside it, cut flat 28 mm above the ball's centre. Its flat top is the ring deck; everything below it, down to the floor of the bowl, is one volume for the electronics.

The stage also carries the hinge towers, the latch pawl, the slot of the ring with the ring pawl, and the collar that centres the lid, and it is what one sees when the lid opens. The deck height leaves 20 mm under the lid for the part of the ring that stands proud.

Origin: realisation of the owner-suggested layout.
