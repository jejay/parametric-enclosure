# Flattened underside: the foot of the ball

The lowest 3 mm of the sphere are cut off. On the resulting flat of 34 mm diameter the ball stands by itself: on the bench, during assembly, and wherever it is used without its tongue. With the lid open the centre of gravity moves 7 mm backwards; a full sphere would roll onto its back, while on the flat the ball stands with 10 mm of margin.

Screwed to the grass tongue, the ball still stands on this flat: the underside of the tongue lies in the same plane, and both stand on the table together.

This departs from the pure sphere of the reference. The flat cannot be seen from the front or from above.

Origin: agent conclusion.
