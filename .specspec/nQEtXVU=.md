# Front module: bezel with slits, lens, light cone, speaker baffle

The bezel is a tube with a slotted face plate and a seat tube that holds the lens from behind and the light cone behind it; the cone carries the LED in a cup at its narrow end. From the rim of the bowl upwards the tube is 0.4 mm smaller, so that band, stage and lid, which come down over it, keep 0.6 mm from it. The chassis carries the baffle ring with the front chamber and the socket for the bezel; a clamp ring presses the speaker against the baffle.

Bounds: the acoustic result and the evenness of the glow are not simulated. Slit width, slit arc and lens thickness are parameters for tuning after a first print.
