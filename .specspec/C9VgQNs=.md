# Recognisable classic Poké Ball exterior

Closed, the device reads as the classic Poké Ball: red upper half, white lower half, black equatorial band and a round front button surrounded by a black rim. The proportions between the halves, the black band, the black rim, the white flat rim inside it and the button are taken from researched reference pictures rather than estimated from memory.

Size: the smaller the better. Roughly 120 mm outer diameter is the absolute maximum.

Origin: owner intention.
