# Ring compartment high in the dome, electronics in one volume

The ring compartment does not need to sit at exactly half level of the Poké Ball. The inside of the ball can be almost a full sphere with only a bit of the top cut off to make space for the ring. This greatly increases the coherent volume for the electronics compared with placing all electronics into one half, which in turn keeps the ball small.

Origin: owner-suggested conclusion.
