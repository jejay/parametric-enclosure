# Building manual with step and explosion renderings

manual.py renders the overview, a labelled section, the exploded view, one or two pictures for each of the twelve assembly steps, and detail views of latch, hinge, ring lock, emergency path, a figure on the grass with a section through the tongue, and print orientation. manual_text.py holds the instructions and the purchasing notes. Position numbers come from the assembly list, so the balloons in every picture match the bill of materials in docs/building_manual.md.

Bounds: wiring is described in docs/electronics.md, not drawn. The instructions have not been tested by building the device.
