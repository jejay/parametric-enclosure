# The owner runs the hunt from their own smartphone

Everything related to the hunt is managed through an app that the owner runs on their own smartphone: hints, the closeness signal and the final trigger that opens the ball.

Current scope boundary: the app and the firmware are not programmed for now. The hardware and its layout must nevertheless be shown to be able to support them.

Origin: owner intention.
