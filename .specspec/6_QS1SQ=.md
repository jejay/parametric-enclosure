# The ball speaks

The device plays speech aloud: either hints for the hunt for the four Pokémon Tonies (the three classic starters Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle, plus Pikachu), or one custom message per Tonie that the owner recorded personally.

Origin: owner intention.
