# A tongue of tall grass in front of the ball carries the Tonie and the reader

A green tongue of "tall grass" is connected to the Poké Ball. Only the tongue: no grass surrounds the ball. Its underside is totally flush with the ball's base, so that the ball sits on its own flat and on the flat side of the grass. The flat, non-grass body of the tongue is two to two and a half times as high as the 6.5 mm it had in revision two, so that it is much more stable; that height stays as it is. The tongue is a bit shorter and a tiny bit narrower than in revision three; a little of that space comes from thinning out the grass. Everything of it is light green, and what can be merged is merged. The RFID reader sits in this grass, and a Tonie stands in the grass in front of the ball to be read. This also removes the risk that the reader is too far from the tag.

The reader is the common, easily sourced blue PN5180 board.

Origin: owner-suggested conclusion, revision two. Tongue only, totally flush and the greater height are the owner's decisions of revision three: the owner really did not like the ring of grass that revision two had laid round the ball. One colour, merged, a bit shorter and a tiny bit narrower are the owner's decisions of revision four; the owner called the change of height well executed and wants it left as it is.
