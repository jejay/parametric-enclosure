# A tongue of tall grass in front of the ball carries the Tonie and the reader

A green tongue of "tall grass" is connected to the Poké Ball. Only the tongue: no grass surrounds the ball. Its underside is totally flush with the ball's base, so that the ball sits on its own flat and on the flat side of the grass. The flat, non-grass body of the tongue may easily be two to two and a half times as high as the 6.5 mm it had in revision two; then it should be much more stable. The RFID reader sits in this grass, and a Tonie stands in the grass in front of the ball to be read. This also removes the risk that the reader is too far from the tag.

The reader is the common, easily sourced blue PN5180 board.

Origin: owner-suggested conclusion, revision two. Tongue only, totally flush and the greater height are the owner's decisions of revision three: the owner really did not like the ring of grass that revision two had laid round the ball.
