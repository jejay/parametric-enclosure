# The bowl: foot, seat for the tongue and cable window

The bowl is cut flat 3 mm above its pole. Four bosses on the inside of its front wall, 30.5 mm from the axis, carry heat-set inserts for the screws of the grass tongue; below each boss a clearance hole runs through the wall. A window in the wall at the front, between 26 and 36 mm from the axis, lets the reader harness in.

The checks report that the ball alone stands on its flat with 10 mm of margin when the lid is open, that the flat and the underside of the tongue lie on one plane, that each screw of the tongue engages 4.5 mm of thread, and that the window passes a plug section of 15.5 x 6.5 mm and ends 2.7 mm below the turf surface.

Bounds: in print orientation the four bosses hang from the dome and need the supports that the inside of the bowl needs anyway.
