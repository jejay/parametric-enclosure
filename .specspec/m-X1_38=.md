# Research notes in research/

Ten notes answer the questions the design depends on: the Tonie tag and its unlock (01), reader modules (02), Poké Ball proportions (03), controller, audio, LED and wake sensor (04), cell, charging and servo (05), ring, springs, fasteners and printing (06), firmware and app feasibility (07), and for the grass tongue the mechanics of the blue reader board (08), the size of the Pokémon Tonies (09), and the look of tall grass with the rules for printing it and the small parts it needs (10). research/README.md indexes them, lists every decision with the candidates that were not chosen and why, and records where the notes disagreed.

Each note places its source URLs next to the claims and labels every value as confirmed by several independent sources, taken from one source, or inferred. Reference pictures with the measuring scripts are in research/reference_images/, quoted datasheets, drawings and source files in research/downloads/.

Bounds: the items that no source could verify are listed at the end of research/README.md, first among them the tag type of the 2026 Pokémon figures and a read at 2 to 3 mm above the blue board. No dimensioned drawing of that board exists; note 08 measured it on photographs.
