# 100 mm ball in the proportions of the official replica

The ball is 100 mm in diameter. Its visible proportions follow the thin-band family measured on the official The Wand Company replica, the Sugimori artwork, Pokémon GO and Smash Bros: band height 0.070 D, black ring 0.34 D, white flat rim 0.225 D, button 0.14 D, band recessed by 0.012 D, and a flat ring face from which the button rises by 0.023 D to end roughly flush with the sphere.

Why 100 mm: revision one found it the smallest diameter that takes an 18650 cell lying across the middle, a 40 mm speaker behind the button, an upright 9 g servo under the ring deck and a reader under the floor. Since revision two the reader lies in the grass tongue, and the lowest 16 mm of the bowl only hold the plugs of its harness. Whether the ball could shrink without the reader has not been examined; the layout above the chassis plate is unchanged.

The thick-band look (0.10 to 0.12 D) of Pokémon Let's Go and of most fan models was not chosen. All ratios are parameters; another diameter needs the layout verified again.
