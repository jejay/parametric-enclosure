# Five single-colour exterior parts with seams on the colour boundaries

The exterior consists of a white bowl, a black band ring, a red lid, a black button bezel and a translucent lens. Every seam runs along a colour boundary of the Poké Ball, where a line is expected anyway: bowl to band, band to lid, bezel to its surroundings.

All parts are PETG and print without supports touching a visible surface: bowl and lid with the rim on the bed, band upside down, bezel face down.

Why not one multi-colour print, although the owner has such a printer: an inlaid band means about a hundred colour changes including black into white, with colour bleed exactly at the most visible line. Why PETG: hinge, latch and spring seats stay preloaded for weeks, and PLA creeps and softens at 55 to 60 °C.

This concerns the ball. The grass tongue adds two parts of its own, both in one light green.
