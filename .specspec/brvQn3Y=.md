# The ring survives a shocked girlfriend

The ring sits very snug and deep, so that it stays in place when a shocked or surprised girlfriend jolts or drops the device. It may additionally be held in place by a special mechanism.

The ring is European size 49 with a diameter of 2.5 mm.
