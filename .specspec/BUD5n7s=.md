# Screws, pins, inserts, a hall switch and a lid spring that the owner can buy or has

Screws of 2.2 mm, such as the 2.2 x 6 and 2.2 x 8 of the building manual, are very hard to come by in Germany. The small screws are M2 screws instead.

Of dowel pins of 3 mm the owner can get 3 x 20 and 3 x 25 only. For the hinge the owner asked whether a pin of 2 x 18 could take the place of the 2 x 20.

The hall switch of Texas Instruments could not be found in Germany either; the owner asked for an alternative that Conrad can deliver quickly.

For heat-set inserts the owner has M3 inserts of 5 mm outer diameter in lengths of 5 and 6 mm, and these are used.

Gutekunst, the maker of the lid spring, does not sell to private customers.

Nothing else on the list of material to buy changes; if something else has to change, the owner is told what.

Since then the owner has bought, and has at hand: machine screws M2 x 4, 6, 8, 12, 16 and 20, M3 x 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 and M4 x 6, 10, 14, 18 and 20, with nuts, washers and spring washers for each size; countersunk cross-head screws M3 x 8, 12, 16, 20 and 25 with heads of 5.5 x 1.65 mm; dowel pins of 3 mm in thirteen lengths from 6 to 50 mm; and stainless dowel pins of 2, 3 and 4 mm, each 8, 10, 12, 16 and 18 mm long.

Nothing new is to be bought any more, except what the local Bauhaus in Braunschweig has; its stock can be looked up on its website.

Origin: owner constraint, revision six; the hardware at hand and the limit to the local Bauhaus on 2026-11-24.
