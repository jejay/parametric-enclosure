# Screws, pins and a hall switch that the owner can buy

Screws of 2.2 mm, such as the 2.2 x 6 and 2.2 x 8 of the building manual, are very hard to come by in Germany. The small screws are M2 screws instead.

Of dowel pins of 3 mm the owner can get 3 x 20 and 3 x 25 only. For the hinge the owner asked whether a pin of 2 x 18 could take the place of the 2 x 20.

The hall switch of Texas Instruments could not be found in Germany either; the owner asked for an alternative that Conrad can deliver quickly.

Nothing else on the list of material to buy changes; if something else has to change, the owner is told what.

Origin: owner constraint, revision six.
