# One removable core, clamped by four screws

All electronics sit on one printed chassis that is wired and tested on the bench and then lowered into the bowl. The black band is lowered over it, the stage with the lid goes on top, and four M3 screws through wells in the stage clamp stage, band lugs and chassis flange onto threaded inserts in the bowl. Two 6-pin plugs under the chassis plate join the core to the reader and the hall switch in the grass tongue; nothing else ties the core to the bowl.

The screws can be reached only with the lid open, so no fastener shows on the closed ball. Removing them gives access to the cell.
