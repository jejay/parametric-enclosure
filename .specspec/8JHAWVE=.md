# One 18650 in a snap cradle, charged through USB-C in the black band

A single unprotected flat-top 18650 lies across the chassis in a printed snap cradle with a spring contact and a button contact. It can be swapped after the four stack screws are undone, and it is charged in place through a TP4056 USB-C module with DW01A protection whose socket sits in the black band, 20° left of the hinge. A shallow scoop in the rim of the bowl below it lets the body of the plug reach the socket.

A TPS61023 boost converter with true disconnect supplies 5 V to servo, reader transmitter and LED and is switched off in sleep.

One cell gives 16 to 22 h of speech, about 63 h of idle Bluetooth, and more than a year asleep, so a second cell is not needed. Wireless charging was rejected: the coil cannot get close enough to a pad in a sphere and its field endangers NFC tags.

A charging socket does not betray that the ball opens; any talking toy has one.

Origin: owner-suggested cell; agent conclusion for the rest (research/05_power_servo.md).
