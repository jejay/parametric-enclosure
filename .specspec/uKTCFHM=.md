# Dimensions in params.py

params.py holds the diameter, the measured ratios of band, ring, flat rim and button, and every other dimension of the device as named values, grouped by function and annotated with the research note they come from.

The round tip of the tongue and the ground that is kept free of grass are derived from the diameter of a Tonie's foot, so one value moves both. Bounds: the outline of the reader board and the position of its antenna, the height of the controller board, the connector overhang of the charger, the servo horn, the Tonie's foot and the ring are assumptions marked MEASURE. They have to be replaced by measurements of the delivered parts before printing.
