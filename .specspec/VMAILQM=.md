# The ball jumps open and presents the ring

When all Tonies have been found, the Poké Ball jumps open and presents the engagement ring. The opening is commanded from the smartphone only.

Suggested means (owner): a spring mechanism released by a servo. A generic, typical 9 g servo is to be used.

Origin: owner intention with an owner-suggested means.
