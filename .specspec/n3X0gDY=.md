# The girlfriend is looking at the front when it opens

To make the girlfriend look at the device from the frontal perspective at the moment it jumps open and presents the ring, the sound leaves the ball on its front. The speaker slits therefore sit on the front of the Poké Ball, possibly integrated into the black inlet rim.

Origin: owner intention (frontal attention) with an owner-suggested conclusion (front speaker slits in the black rim).
