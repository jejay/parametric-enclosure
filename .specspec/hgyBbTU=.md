# What the verification covers, and what it cannot

The checks establish: each printed part is one valid, watertight solid; no two of the 42 items overlap in the closed assembly except declared threads and press fits; critical clearances hold; the lid passes through its travel in 2.5° steps without contact and is stopped at the end; the latch holds when engaged and frees the lid when released; pawl, servo arm and lock slider move freely; the ring is retained when locked and free when released; the emergency path is unobstructed; core, band and stage can be put together in the order of the manual; the ball is self-righting, stands with the lid open, and the spring beats gravity. For the grass tongue they establish: the ball rests on four pads and can be lowered onto the tongue; the underside of the tongue lies in the plane of the ball's flat; reader, hall switch and turf go together; a plug passes the hidden cable window; each of the four Pokémon figures on the spot clears the ball; the standing surface is plain, with the grass at a distance and the front open; no tuft of grass has a twin on the other side; and the device stands on flat and tongue with the centre of gravity at least 27 mm inside the edge.

They do not establish that a Tonie's tag is reached from 3 mm below its foot, or with metal under the tongue, or that its magnets are those the hall switch is laid out for, that the printed parts have these dimensions, that the spring delivers its catalogue torque, or how the speaker sounds. Passing checks are evidence about geometry only.
