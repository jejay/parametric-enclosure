# What the verification covers, and what it cannot

The checks establish: each printed part is one valid, watertight solid; no two of the 43 items overlap in the closed assembly except declared threads and press fits; critical clearances hold; the lid passes through its travel in 2.5° steps without contact and is stopped at the end; the latch holds when engaged and frees the lid when released; latch pawl, servo arm and ring pawl move freely; the ring is retained when locked and free when released; the emergency path is unobstructed; core, band and stage can be put together in the order of the manual; the hinge towers enclose their pins and the sleeve between the ribs of the lid keeps them; the hinge pins and the lock pin can be put in, a screwdriver and pliers reach the hinge pins, the spring's forward leg can be lifted onto its pin, and a hex key reaches the screws inside the core; every M2 screw has 4 mm of thread in the print, and the printed washer keeps the pawl on its pivot; the ball is self-righting, stands with the lid open, and the spring beats gravity. For a lid whose surfaces lie 0.2 mm further out than drawn and which stands as askew as its hinge lets it they establish: nothing closes in on it over the first 8° of opening and nothing touches it from there to the stop; the released pawl lets its hook pass, the pawl at rest holds it and comes back over a tooth that stands high; and the hook leaves its slot and finds it again from 2.9 mm beside its place, over slopes only. For the grass tongue they establish: the ball rests on four pads and can be lowered onto the tongue; the underside of the tongue lies in the plane of the ball's flat; reader, hall switch and grass top go together, and the rim of the tray holds the tip of the grass top; a plug passes the hidden cable window; each of the four Pokémon figures on the tongue clears the ball; the standing surface is plain and unmarked, with all grass behind the figure; the grass is thin, stands in the two corners beside the ball and has no twins left and right; the turf screws end inside tuft roots; and the device stands on flat and tongue with the centre of gravity at least 24 mm inside the edge.

They do not establish that a Tonie's tag is reached from 3 mm below its foot, or with metal under the tongue, or that its magnets are those the hall switch is laid out for, or that a figure is put down close enough to the unmarked spot, that the printed parts have these dimensions, in particular the holes for screws and pins, or lie within the 0.2 mm that the checks of play allow them, that the parts slide on each other as easily as slopes suggest, since no check knows friction, how firmly a pin holds in the sleeve, that the spring delivers its catalogue torque, or how the speaker sounds. Passing checks are evidence about geometry only.
