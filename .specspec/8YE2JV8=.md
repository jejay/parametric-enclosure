# The ball stays upright when a Tonie is presented

Presenting a Tonie must not make anyone turn the ball over. The first design read a Tonie at the underside of the ball. The owner judged the risk high that the girlfriend would then hold the ball upside down and that the lid would open towards the ground, which would be totally confusing.

Origin: owner intention, revision two.
