# The lid works whatever its hinge lets it do, and whatever the printer makes of the parts

A printed hinge is not one-dimensional. It has room in every direction and at every angle, so the lid can stand askew, at least a little. A lid that stands askew must not be caught: not by the latch whose pawl has just been moved out of its way, and not anywhere else.

Printing is not precise either, and precision cannot be taken for granted, so the margins have to be large. Everything that touches while the lid opens and closes is gone through and tested with the lid a little off in every direction and at every angle; nothing is assumed to move only in the direction it is meant to. The lid and its opening mechanism are to work smoothly, with room for error and tolerances.

Origin: owner intention, 2026-11-24, after the first print of lid, stage and band.
