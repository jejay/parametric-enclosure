# The ring stands in a felt-lined slot and a steel pin passes through it

The ring stands upright, facing the front, 8.6 mm deep in the slot of an exchangeable cradle. The slot is lined with felt until the ring holds by friction. A 3 mm steel dowel pin slides through the cradle and through the ring, 0.3 mm above the inside of the band. Locked, the ring cannot be lifted more than that, whatever happens to the ball. A slider behind the ring withdraws the pin by 7 mm.

The cradle is a separate small part because the ring's exact width, thickness and setting height are not yet measured. They are parameters, and only the cradle has to be printed again.

Origin: agent conclusion for the owner's special mechanism (research/06 section 1).
