# One front module for sound and light

The black ring around the button is a printed bezel tube. Four arc slits in its face, on the diagonals, let the sound out (134 mm², 16 % of the cone area). Behind it a Visaton K 40 speaker sits on a baffle with a 2 mm front chamber, driven by a MAX98357A amplifier from the battery rail.

In the centre of the same tube a translucent lens forms the white flat rim and the button. One WS2812B LED lights it through a white cone that keeps the light out of the sound path.

Why the K 40: 83 dB at 4.3 mm depth; the 28 mm alternative is 8 dB quieter. Its axis sits 4 mm below the button axis to leave room for the latch above it.

The slit area is below the 20 % that the grille guidance recommends. The consequence is a front-cavity resonance in the upper speech band. It is accepted for speech and can be raised by widening the slits.

Origin: owner-suggested slits in the black rim; agent conclusion for the rest (research/04_mcu_audio_led.md sections 3 and 4, research/06 section 8).
