# Exterior parts: bowl, band, lid, bezel, lens

parts_shell.py builds the five exterior parts. The bowl carries the insert bosses and the notch for the bezel, and since revision three the four bosses and the cable window for the grass tongue; the band carries the lid seat, the lugs that the stack screws clamp, the skirt that centres it in the bowl and the notch for the USB-C socket; the lid carries its stiffening lip. The same parts are exported in print orientation to export/stl/.

Bounds: seam widths and the colour match of the filaments can only be judged on printed parts.
