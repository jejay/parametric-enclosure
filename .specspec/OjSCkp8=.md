# A complete schematic of the electronics

The electronics manual carries a schematic of the whole circuit. It shows every pin of every board with its name, the pins that are left out as well, and every resistor, capacitor and diode. Drawing it includes checking that the circuit makes sense and is fit for its purpose.

Origin: owner intention, 2026-11-26. The owner also asked that the drawing be checked by rendering it with resvg.
