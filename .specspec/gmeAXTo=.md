# The bowl is cut for the owner's inserts: holes of 4.4 mm, 6 mm inserts at the rim, 5 mm inserts below

The eight insert holes of the bowl are 4.4 mm wide, for inserts that measure 5.0 mm over the knurl; 2.0 mm of wall remain round each hole, where 1.3 mm are asked for. The earlier holes of 4.0 mm were for inserts of 4.6 mm.

The 6 mm inserts go into the four bosses at the rim, where the stack screws enter from above and find 6.0 mm of thread. The 5 mm inserts go into the four bosses low on the front wall: the screws of the tongue enter them from below, find 3.8 mm of thread and pull the inserts into their holes, and under a 6 mm insert only 0.2 mm would have been left for the plastic that is pushed aside.

The hole size is the one published for another insert of the same diameter, not one stated for these inserts, so a test boss with both holes is exported and the manual has an insert tried in it before the bowl is printed.
