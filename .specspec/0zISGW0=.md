# Researched and cross-checked part choices

Part compatibility is researched very thoroughly before parts are chosen: dimensions, and functional questions such as whether the RFID reader can actually read Tonie RFID tags. Compatibility and dimensions are validated with multiple independent sources.

The research is stored in this repository, including component candidates that do not make it into the final design.
