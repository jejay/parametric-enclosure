Technical Specifications for Toniebox 2

    • Wi-Fi® 802.11 a/b/g/n radio:

    • Maximum transmission power: 20 dBm EIRP

    • 2.4 GHz Band: 2400 - 2483.5 MHz

      • US Channels 1 - 11

      • EU Channels 1 - 13

    • 5 GHz Band: 5150 - 5850 MHz

      • Channels 36, 40, 44, 48

    • Bluetooth® 5.2-5.3 radio:

    • Maximum transmission power: 10 dBm EIRP

    • 2.4 GHz Band: 2400 - 2480 MHz

    • NFC: 

      • Maximum H-Field in 10m distance: 9.24-20.4 dBμA/m

      • Frequency: 13.56 mHz

    • External power supply: 5 V ⎓ max. 1.6 A 

    • Power consumption in off-mode: ≤ 0.3W (activated after 10 minutes automatically)

    Charging Instructions:

    • The Toniebox 2 must only be charged using a certified Class II power supply from a power source with limited output in accordance with EN 61558-2-7 (marked with the symbol in accordance with IEC 60417) with a maximum rated output of 5 volts direct current, maximum 1.6 A. Only charge the Toniebox 2 with the enclosed cable or with compatible charging accessories from the Tonies range. The use of incompatible accessories may cause fire, electric shock, or damage to the Toniebox 2 or accessories and may result in injury.

    • The power delivered by the charger must be between a minimum of 5 watts required by the radio equipment and a maximum of 15 watts in order to achieve the maximum charging speed.
