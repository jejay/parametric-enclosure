tonies® x Pokémon FAQ

 
 
 

Gotta catch 'em all! ⚡️🍃🔥💧

We're so excited to welcome Pokémon to the tonies® family! Whether you're a long-time Pokémon fan or a little Trainer just starting your adventure, we've put together some of the most frequently asked questions to help you get ready to catch them all.

Check out the FAQs below for everything you need to know about our Pokémon Tonies...

Is this official Pokémon content that was developed with Pokémon?

As official as it gets! We teamed up with Pikachu Press to develop the content for these Tonies, from start to finish.

Can I pre-order the Pokémon Tonies?

Pre-order is now open! Catch all four Pokémon Tonies at tonies.com  and select retailers.⚡️

Just a heads-up – if you bundle them with other Tonies, they'll all ship together when your pre-order is ready.

Why do Pokémon Tonies have a black base?

Great question! The black base was designed to keep the focus on the Pokémon itself. The clean background lets the Pokémon be the hero of the Tonie.

Are the Pokémon Tonies and the yellow Toniebox limited edition?

No, neither the Pokémon Tonies nor the yellow Toniebox 2 are limited edition. The TB2 is a unique Pokémon-inspired design and the Pokémon Tonies will join our standard lineup!

Why is the music not the original Pokémon music?

The music was created with the Pokémon team, built to drive the storytelling and give little Trainers the best listening experience. We love how it turned out and hope you do too!

Do these Tonies feature the genuine Pokémon voices?

They do! We also have new voices for narrators and announcers.

Is the content on a Pokémon Tonie based on the TV show?

Nope. All the content you'll hear is completely original, made in partnership with our friends over at Pokémon.

Will Pikachu be sold separately?

Yes, you can buy all four of our Pokémon Tonies (Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle) separately!

Do the Pokémon Tonies only work with the Yellow Toniebox 2?

The Pokémon Tonies work with all Tonieboxes!

We hope this helps! If you have any further questions, don't forget to drop our Customer Happiness team a message HERE.
