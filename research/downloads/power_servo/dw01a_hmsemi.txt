General Description                                   Ordering Information

The DW01A battery protection IC is designed to        DW01A
protect lithium-ion/polymer battery from damage or
degrading the lifetime due to overcharge,                    PACKAGE TYPE
overdischarge, and/or overcurrent for one-cell               SOT-23-6
lithium-ion/polymer battery powered systems, such as
cellular phones.                                             TEMPERATURE RANGE
                                                             -40°C~+85°C
The ultra-small package and less required external
components make it ideal to integrate the DW01A              OVERCHARGE PROTECTION
into the limited space of battery pack. The accurate         4.3V± 50mV
±50mV overcharging detection voltage ensures safe
and full utilization charging. The very low standby
current drains little current from the cell while in
storage.

     Package SOT-23-6.                                     Lithium-Polymer Battery Pack

     Ultra-Low Quiescent Current at 3A
     (Vcc=3.6V).

     Overdischarge Current at 4 A
     (Vcc=1.8 V).

     Precision Overcharge Protection Voltage
     4.3V ± 50mV

     Two Detection Levels for Overcurrent
     Protection.

     Delay times are generated by internal circuits.
     No external capacitors required.
